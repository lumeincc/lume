# Name and logo

LUME's source code is free software under the GNU Affero General Public
License v3.0. **The LUME name and logo are not.** They are the designations
LUME Inc. uses to identify its products; the logo is also a work whose
exclusive rights belong to LUME Inc. They are covered by
[`LICENSES/LicenseRef-LUME-Brand.txt`](LICENSES/LicenseRef-LUME-Brand.txt).

The split protects the people who use LUME. A messenger is trusted for its
protection, and anyone who installs an app called LUME with the LUME logo must
be able to rely on it being LUME Inc.'s build, not a modified copy that could
record messages.

## You may

- Fork, modify and distribute the code under the AGPL.
- Say that your project is "based on LUME" or "a fork of LUME".
- Use the name in text when referring to the official project: in articles,
  reviews, talks and compatibility notes.
- Show the unmodified logo when pointing to the official project.

## You may not

- Publish a modified build under the LUME name or with the LUME logo — in an
  app store or anywhere else.
- Use a name or logo for a messaging product that could be confused with
  LUME.
- Suggest that LUME Inc. made, endorses or supports your build.

A fork needs its own name and its own icon. That is the whole requirement.
