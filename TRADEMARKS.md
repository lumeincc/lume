3. The AGPL grants rights in the code. It grants no right to use the LUME name

## How LUME Inc. enforces these rules

If the name, the logo or the artwork is used in breach of the brand licence or
this page, LUME Inc. may, as the law allows:

1. ask the person responsible to stop the use, or to change the name, icon or
   statement concerned;
2. ask app stores, hosting providers, domain name registrars and other
   platforms to remove or suspend the product, page or account under their own
   rules;
3. take the measures the [Terms of Use](legal/TERMS.md) allow against an
   account whose username misuses the name, such as suspending or deleting
   the account and barring the username once the account is deleted;
4. bring a claim in court or a complaint to the competent authorities for
   copyright infringement, breach of the brand licence, unfair competition and,
   once a mark is registered, trademark infringement, and seek an order to stop
   the use, the removal of infringing copies and compensation for its losses.

To the extent the law allows, if LUME Inc. tolerates a use or does not act at
once, that does not make the use permitted and does not waive any of its
rights.
