   company name, and on the law against unfair competition and misleading
   designations. Once a registration is granted, LUME Inc. also relies on
   trademark law in the country of that registration.
2. The brand licence grants only the rights listed in it. LUME Inc. reserves
   every other right in the name, the logo and the artwork.
3. The AGPL grants rights in the code. It grants no right to use the LUME name
   or logo, and a permitted use gives you no rights in them.

## You may

1. Copy, study, modify, reverse engineer and distribute the code under the
   AGPL. Nothing on this page limits the rights the AGPL gives you in the code.
2. Keep the word LUME where it appears inside the code, for example in
   identifiers, file names and protocol labels such as `LUME/v1/`. That is part
   of the code, not the name of your product.
3. Refer to the LUME project by name in text, for example in articles,
   reviews, talks, tutorials and compatibility statements, as long as what you
   say is accurate and does not suggest that LUME Inc. endorses you. Use the
   name in plain text, not styled to look like the logo, and no more than you
   need to refer to the project.
4. Say in the description of your project, not in its name, that it is
   "based on LUME" or "a fork of LUME", and say that it is not made or endorsed

5. Display the name and logo unmodified in this repository and in unmodified
   copies of it, to identify the official LUME project.

A fork needs its own name and its own icon. Before you distribute a modified
version, give it its own name and icon, and remove the LUME logo and the
artwork in `brand/` from it. Keep the copyright and licence notices that the
AGPL requires. This concerns only the name and the artwork; what you may do
with the code is set by the AGPL alone.

1. LUME Inc. may mark the name and logo with ™ to show that it claims them as
   its trademarks.
2. The symbol ® and the words "registered trademark" are used only for a mark
   registered in the country concerned. Do not use them with LUME.
3. You do not need to add ™ when you refer to LUME in text.

## How LUME Inc. enforces these rules

If the name, the logo or the artwork is used in breach of the brand licence or
this page, LUME Inc. may, as the law allows:

1. ask the person responsible to stop the use, or to change the name, icon or
   statement concerned;
2. ask app stores, hosting providers, domain name registrars and other
   platforms to remove or suspend the product, page or account under their own
   rules;
3. free or bar a LUME username that misuses the name, under the
   [Terms of Use](legal/TERMS.md);
4. bring a claim in court or a complaint to the competent authorities for
   copyright infringement, breach of the brand licence, unfair competition and,
   once a mark is registered, trademark infringement, and seek an order to stop
   the use, the removal of infringing copies and compensation for its losses.

To the extent the law allows, if LUME Inc. tolerates a use or does not act at
once, that does not make the use permitted and does not waive any of its
rights.
