are covered by [`LICENSES/LicenseRef-LUME-Brand.txt`](LICENSES/LicenseRef-LUME-Brand.txt).

Қазақша нұсқа: [TRADEMARKS.kk.md](TRADEMARKS.kk.md).

The split exists to protect people who use LUME. A messenger is trusted for its
security, and someone who installs an app called LUME, with the LUME logo, has
to be able to rely on it being the build LUME Inc. publishes — not a modified
copy that might log messages.

## You may

- Fork, modify and redistribute the code under the AGPL.
- Say that your project is "based on LUME" or "a fork of LUME".
- Use the name in text to refer to the official project: articles, reviews,
  talks, compatibility notes.
- Show the unmodified logo when you are pointing at the official project.

## You may not

- Publish a modified build under the name LUME or with the LUME logo, in an app
  store or anywhere else.
- Use a name or logo confusingly similar to LUME's for a messaging product.
- Suggest that LUME Inc. made, endorses or supports your build.

A fork needs its own name and its own icon. That is the whole requirement.

Ask LUME Inc. before doing anything this page does not clearly allow.
