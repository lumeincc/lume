# Application for the LUME trademark in the Republic of Kazakhstan

A working paper for filing an application with the RSE "National Institute of
Intellectual Property" of the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Kazakhstan
(NIIP, kazpatent.kz). Prices follow the NIIP fees in force from 1 January 2026,
in tenge including VAT; check them on the
[fees page](https://kazpatent.kz/ru/ip-objects/trademark/applicants/quny)
before paying.

## 1. What to register

| No. | Sign | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LUME | word mark, standard font, no colour | The widest protection of the name: it covers any font and styling. File it first |
| 2 | LUME logo (the ring) | figurative mark, file from `brand/` | Protects the drawing itself. File it as a separate application: one application covers one mark |

The applicant and future owner is LUME Inc. (ТОО «LUME Inc.»), BIN 260740032421.
The mark is registered to the company, not to the director, so that it stays
with the company whatever happens to its membership and can be licensed.

## 2. Nice classes and lists of goods and services

The base examination fee covers **up to 3 classes** and **up to 10 items in
each class**; every item beyond ten in a class costs extra. Each class therefore
has ten items or fewer. Items are separated by semicolons, as the
[NIIP information letter](https://kazpatent.kz/ru/75/ads) recommends. Before
filing, check each item against the Alphabetical List of the current edition of
the Nice Classification and copy its wording from there word for word.

**Class 9: software.**
downloadable mobile applications; downloadable computer software; downloadable
instant messaging software; encryption software; user authentication software;
downloadable videoconferencing software; data backup software; computer
software platforms, recorded or downloadable.

**Class 38: telecommunications.**
message sending; computer-aided transmission of messages and images; instant
messaging services; providing internet chatrooms; transmission of digital
files; videoconferencing services; teleconferencing services; transmission of
voice messages; providing access to global computer networks; streaming of
data.

**Class 42: software and data protection.**
software development; software as a service [SaaS]; encryption, decryption and
authentication of information, messages and data; services for protecting data
against unauthorised access; electronic data storage; data security
consultancy; maintenance of computer software; updating of computer software;
hosting computer sites [websites]; research in the field of encryption
technology.

Why these classes: 9 covers the app itself, 38 covers message delivery, which
is what the relay does, and 42 covers encryption and the services around it.
All three fall within the base fee. Paid subscriptions, if they ever exist, and
branded merchandise (classes 25 and 16) are not included; they are added in a
separate application once they actually exist.

## 3. Cost of one application

LUME Inc. is a small business; before paying, check that the NIIP applies
the fee for small and medium-sized businesses.

| Stage | Small or medium-sized business | Legal entity without discount |
|---|---|---|
| Filing and examination (preliminary and full), up to 3 classes and 10 items in each | 88,359.38 | 117,812.50 |
| Registration, issue of the certificate and publication, up to 3 classes | 37,849.64 | 37,849.64 |
| **Total for one mark** | **126,209.02** | **155,662.14** |

Two marks (the word and the logo) cost twice as much. The fees page gives a
single registration fee, not split by category; ask the NIIP whether the small
and medium-sized business discount applies to it. Renewal after 10 years costs
164,126.56 for a small or medium-sized business. The services of a patent
attorney, if one is hired, are paid separately.

## 4. Procedure

1. **Search.** Before filing, check for identical marks, or marks similar to
   the point of confusion, in classes 9, 38 and 42: in the State Register of
   Trademarks of the Republic of Kazakhstan on kazpatent.kz, and in the WIPO
   Global Brand Database (branddb.wipo.int) for marks protected in Kazakhstan
   under the Madrid System. Search not only for LUME but also for names that
   sound or look similar (LUMA, LUMI, LUMEN, and in Cyrillic ЛЮМ, ЛЮМЕ). Record
   the result here. If a similar mark turns up in the same classes, decide
   whether to file together with a patent attorney.
2. **Filing.** Electronically through the NIIP personal account
   ([newcab.kazpatent.kz](https://newcab.kazpatent.kz/)), signed with the
   company's digital signature; or through a patent attorney under a power of
   attorney. The language is Russian or Kazakh. For the logo, attach an image
   of the mark.
3. **Payment** of the examination fee: on filing, through the payment gateway
   of the personal account. It is not refunded if the application is refused.
4. **Preliminary examination**: within 1 month of the date the application is
   received; a notice that the application has been accepted for examination
   follows.
5. **Full examination**: within 7 months of the filing date. Answer requests
   from the examiners within the time they set, or the application is treated
   as withdrawn.
6. **Decision to register** → pay the registration fee → entry in the register
   and issue of the certificate within 10 working days.
7. The registration is valid for **10 years from the filing date** and can be
   renewed for 10 years on a request filed in the last year of the term.

## 5. What to do after filing

- Enter the application number in [`TRADEMARKS.md`](../../TRADEMARKS.md) in
  place of the "To be filled in before release" note; after registration, the
  certificate number.
- Until the mark is registered, **do not put ®** next to LUME, and do not use
  the words "тауар белгісі", "товарный знак" or "зарегистрированный товарный
  знак" for it: under article 20 of Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan
  No. 456 on trademarks, only the holder of a certificate may use these
  warning marks. ™ may be used.
- Within 6 months of the filing date, applications can be filed in other
  countries claiming the priority of the Kazakh application (Paris Convention),
  or an international application can be filed under the Madrid System through
  the NIIP, naming the countries needed (the European Union, Uzbekistan,
  Kyrgyzstan and others). Russia only after a decision on item 4 of the
  [open decisions](DECISIONS.md).
- Use the mark as it is registered: an unregistered change of spelling weakens
  the protection, and a mark not used for three years in a row can be
  terminated early at the request of an interested party.
