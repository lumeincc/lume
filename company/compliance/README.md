# company/compliance/

Internal papers LUME Inc. keeps to meet the law of the Republic of Kazakhstan on
personal data, and the EU rules where they apply. They are not user-facing; the
user-facing policies are in [`legal/`](../../legal/README.md).

| Paper | What it is for |
|---|---|
| [ORDER-PERSONAL-DATA.ru.md](ORDER-PERSONAL-DATA.ru.md) | The director's order approving the personal-data policy and the list of personal data, and appointing the person responsible for organising processing (Law No. 94-V, article 25(2) sub-paragraphs 1, 1-1 and 10). Its appendix doubles as the GDPR article 30 record of processing |
| [BREACH-RESPONSE.ru.md](BREACH-RESPONSE.ru.md) | What to do, and by when, if personal-data security is breached (article 22, article 25(2) sub-paragraph 8, the authorised body's notification rules; GDPR articles 33 and 34) |
| [DECISIONS.ru.md](DECISIONS.ru.md) | Legal decisions that are still open and block the first public release |

The orders are templates: they take effect when the director signs them. Names
and signatures are never committed here.
