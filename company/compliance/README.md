# company/compliance/

Internal papers LUME Inc. (ТОО «LUME Inc.») keeps to meet the law of the
Republic of Kazakhstan on personal data, and the EU rules where they apply. They
are not user-facing; the user-facing policies are in
[`legal/`](../../legal/README.md).

| Paper | What it is for |
|---|---|
| [ORDER-PERSONAL-DATA.md](ORDER-PERSONAL-DATA.md) | The director's order approving the personal-data policy and the list of personal data, and appointing the person responsible for organising processing (Law No. 94-V, article 25(2) sub-paragraphs 1, 1-1 and 10). Its appendix lists the data on the relay server and the data received outside it, and doubles as the GDPR article 30 record of processing |
| [BREACH-RESPONSE.md](BREACH-RESPONSE.md) | What to do, and by when, if personal-data security is breached (article 22, article 25(2) sub-paragraph 8, the authorised body's notification rules; GDPR articles 33 and 34) |
| [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) | Legal decisions that block the first public release, and the work that settled decisions still need |
| [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md) | The trademark application for LUME in Kazakhstan: which marks, classes and lists of goods and services, fees, procedure and what to do after filing |

The papers are published in English. A paper that must be signed or filed in
Kazakhstan, such as the order or the trademark application, is signed or filed
in the form and language the law or the receiving body requires.

The order is a template: it takes effect when the director signs it. Names and
signatures are never committed here.
