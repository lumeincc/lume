# Open legal decisions

Decisions that must be made before LUME is released to the public. Each one is
closed by a decision of the director, with a lawyer admitted to practise in the
Republic of Kazakhstan where needed. Once a decision is made, its outcome goes
into the documents in `legal/`. A row marked "Settled" or "Decided" stays until
the work it still needs is done, and is then removed.

| No. | Question | Why it matters | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Contract with the data centre** | Settled: the server will be hosted in a data centre in Kazakhstan. The provider processes data on behalf of LUME Inc. (ТОО «LUME Inc.») and must be bound by a contract | A contract with terms on processing personal data on the company's behalf, confidentiality, security measures, access to the equipment and incident notification; then enter the provider's name in `legal/PRIVACY.md` |
| 2 | **Data subject in the consent without a full name** | Paragraph 4 of article 8 of Law No. 94-V lists the subject's full name among the details of a consent; LUME does not collect names | A legal opinion that a subject identified by username and key is enough, or another method "that makes it possible to confirm that consent was obtained" |
| 3 | **Proof of consent** | Settled: sub-paragraph 5) of paragraph 2 of article 25 requires the operator to prove consent. `Register` carries the number of the version of the Terms of Use, the Privacy Policy and the Consent the user accepted, `AccountData` tells the app the version in effect, `AcceptTerms` records a later version, and the relay stores the number and the day in the `accounts` table; it refuses a registration with an outdated version (`docs/PROTOCOL.md` §4.2, §5 and §7). The relay keeps the number and the day, not the user's signature | A lawyer confirms that this record, with the published text of each numbered version, is enough proof of consent and of the conclusion of the contract, including for accounts registered with software other than the official apps, where registering is acceptance by conduct (article 396(3) of the Civil Code). Keep every published version of the documents with its number |
| 4 | **Russia and RuStore** | Distribution in Russia may lead Roskomnadzor to require registration as an organiser of information distribution (Federal Law No. 149-FZ, article 10.1): storing metadata in Russia, identifying users and giving the FSB the means to decrypt. This cannot be reconciled with end-to-end encryption, and refusal leads to blocking. Federal Law No. 152-FZ also requires the data of Russian citizens to be stored in Russia | Do not publish in RuStore and do not target Russia (prices, domain, advertising) until a separate decision is made with a lawyer |
| 5 | **EU representative** | GDPR article 27 and DSA article 13 require a representative for a service from outside the EU that is offered to users in the EU; a messenger in continuous use does not fall under the exemption for "occasional" processing | Appoint a representative (specialised firms provide this service) before LUME is opened to the EU, or do not offer LUME in the EU until then |
| 6 | **LUME trademark** | Without registration the name is less well protected; `TRADEMARKS.md` describes the name and logo as trademarks used by LUME Inc. and not yet registered | File an application with the NIIP for the word mark LUME in classes 9, 38 and 42 (about KZT 126,000 for a small or medium-sized business), then for the logo; decide on international registration within 6 months. Everything is prepared in [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md) |
| 7 | **Contact addresses** | The policies refer to addresses that do not exist yet; the DSA requires single points of contact for authorities and users | Set up separate addresses: personal data, child safety, public authorities, complaints and claims, permission requests and misuse of the name and logo, security reports from people without a GitHub account, and general |
| 8 | **Website: hosting and logs** | Settled: the website is on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses itself, as an independent operator; LUME Inc. receives no logs. The site sets no cookies and uses no local or session storage (`legal/WEBSITE.md`) | A lawyer confirms that with this hosting the requirement to store visitors' IP addresses in Kazakhstan does not apply to the company; if it does, move the site to a server in a data centre in Kazakhstan |
| 9 | **Notifying the authorised body (article 10-1)** | Small and medium-sized operators (up to 500,000 unique data subjects) are exempt; processing data of restricted access raises the category by one level (article 25-1) | Do not notify for now; track the number of accounts and file the notification before the threshold is reached |
| 10 | **Law "On Online Platforms and Online Advertising"** | The research found that the Law applies to LUME: article 1(7) defines an instant messaging service and article 1(10) includes it among online platforms. Article 2(3) excludes relations between users who use a messenger for personal and family needs, but not the owner's own duties | A lawyer confirms the finding. The owner's duties are tracked in rows 13 and 16 |
| 11 | **Language of the documents** | The documents are published in English | A lawyer confirms that this is enough together with the summary in the app (row 13) |
| 12 | **Legal review of the whole set** | The documents are written as drafts | A review by a lawyer admitted to practise in Kazakhstan before the first public release |
| 13 | **Summary in Kazakh and Russian in the app** | Decided: the documents are published in English. Article 5(1) of the Online Platforms Law requires the user agreement and a short summary of its main terms to be available in Kazakh; articles 24 to 26 of the consumer protection law require information about the service and the provider in Kazakh and Russian | Before release, build the screen on which the user accepts so that it shows a short summary of the main terms of the Terms of Use, the Privacy Policy and the Consent in Kazakh and Russian, for information; the English documents govern. A lawyer confirms that this meets the Online Platforms Law and the consumer protection law |
| 14 | **Minimum age** | Decided: 18 for everyone, everywhere. Under article 22 of the Civil Code, a minor aged 14 to 18 generally needs a legal representative's written consent to enter into a transaction, and the app has no way to collect it | State 18 in every document that gives an age. The screen on which the user accepts asks the user to confirm that they are 18 or older. Set the target audience and the age rating in the app stores to adults |
| 15 | **Deleting an account without the app, and other signed requests** | Google Play requires a way to ask for deletion without reinstalling the app. The relay deletes an account only on a request signed with the account's key (`docs/PROTOCOL.md` §4.2), so a request by e-mail cannot delete an account by itself. LUME Inc. also acts on other requests about an account, such as a request for a full copy of its data or for the facts behind a measure, only if the writer shows with the account's key that the account is theirs (`legal/PRIVACY.md` section 9.4; `legal/TERMS.md` clauses 6.8 and 15.3), and nothing signs such a request yet | Before release, build a small signing tool that derives the identity key from the recovery phrase, sends a signed deletion request without the app, and signs other requests about the account, or a way to sign them in the app. Until then, `legal/ACCOUNT-DELETION.md`, `legal/PRIVACY.md` and `legal/TERMS.md` state the limit |
| 16 | **Online Platforms Law: the owner's duties** | Article 9(3): count users in Kazakhstan, offer a complaint function with an automatic acknowledgement, and use no misleading interface. Article 9(4): consider a request of an authority within 24 hours, and publish the average monthly number of users in Kazakhstan for the last six months. Article 11: answer a complaint with reasons within 20 calendar days. Article 13(2): publish an annual report on systemic risks and moderation. Article 9(1): a legal representative above 100,000 average daily users in Kazakhstan | Decide how users in Kazakhstan are counted when the relay keeps no addresses; who receives and answers authority requests within 24 hours, including on non-working days; how complaints are acknowledged; and who writes the annual report. See the box below |
| 17 | **EU e-Evidence representative** | Regulation (EU) 2023/1543 and Directive (EU) 2023/1544 require a service offered in the EU to designate an establishment or a legal representative to receive European production and preservation orders and to answer them | Designate the representative, and write a procedure for handling orders, before LUME is offered in the EU; together with row 5 |
| 18 | **ePrivacy and EECC** | For users in the EU, LUME is a number-independent interpersonal communications service under the European Electronic Communications Code (Directive (EU) 2018/1972). The ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC), as implemented in each Member State, then governs the confidentiality of communications, traffic data and security incidents, alongside the GDPR | A lawyer's analysis before LUME is offered in the EU: which national rules apply, whether the processing of addresses and network hashes for security is permitted under them, and whether their incident notification rules apply |
| 19 | **Data protection impact assessment** | GDPR article 35 requires one where processing is likely to result in a high risk, as large-scale processing of communications data is likely to be. Article 25(2) sub-paragraph 2) of the Law requires legal, organisational and technical measures | Carry out an assessment before release, covering the relay, the opening of reports, preserved evidence and measures on accounts, and keep it here |
| 20 | **Evidence of child sexual exploitation** | LUME Inc. receives such material in reports, keeps it as evidence and hands it to the authorities. Kazakh criminal law punishes the circulation of such material, and no statute gives a safe harbour for doing this | A lawyer's opinion and an internal procedure for receiving, holding and handing over the material; which Kazakh body receives reports; whether to report also to a recognised foreign body, which is a cross-border transfer under article 16 of the Law; where the report key is held and where staff open reports |
| 21 | **Court for business disputes** | The Terms of Use choose the courts of the Republic of Kazakhstan at LUME Inc.'s location (article 32 of the Civil Procedure Code). For a dispute with a business user that court must be named precisely; consumers keep the courts the law gives them (article 30(9)) | A lawyer names the court with jurisdiction over Temirtau for disputes between businesses, and the clause is checked against it |
| 22 | **Data held outside the server** | The data in section B of appendix 1 to the [order](ORDER-PERSONAL-DATA.md) is personal data held outside the relay, including correspondence (letters to the registered office among it), claims, notices and appeals, requests about personal data, opened reports, preserved evidence, the record of authority requests, the record of measures, and the copy LUME Inc. keeps of each vulnerability report (rows 18 to 26) | Choose where each is stored, in Kazakhstan under article 12(2), with any copy abroad only as article 16 allows and as section 7 of the Privacy Policy lists; the e-mail service and the retention periods; then fill in the order and the Privacy Policy |
| 23 | **Post before the addresses are published** | Until the contact addresses are published, the contact page tells people to send claims, requests about personal data and any other formal communication by post to the registered office in Temirtau, whose address is recorded in the state database "Legal Entities" under BIN 260740032421 and can be obtained through the eGov portal as information about a registered legal entity. The website page is already in effect and sends claimants there (`legal/WEBSITE.md`, sections 4.4 and 10.2) | Make sure post sent to the registered office is received, passed on to the people who answer it and handled as section 4.4 of the website page says. People outside Kazakhstan usually cannot use the eGov portal, so publish the address for claims (row 7) as soon as it exists |
