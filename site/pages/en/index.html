01What LUME is

A messenger that cannot read your conversations

For Android and Windows. Built so that the people who run it cannot read what you write or see who writes to whom.

Your account is a key

No phone number, no e-mail, no name. Your account is created on your device from a 24-word recovery phrase that is never sent to us.

Locked on your device

Messages are encrypted on the sender's device and decrypted on the recipient's. The server that carries them never has the keys.

Ready for quantum computers

Every conversation starts with two key exchanges at once, classical X25519 and post-quantum ML-KEM-768. The result stays secret as long as either of them holds.

Open to inspection

All the code is public under the GNU AGPL. It is built from libsodium and liboqs, invents no cryptography of its own, and is tested against published vectors.

02Honest about protection

What it protects, and what it does not

A messenger that overstates its security puts people at risk. These lists come from the specification, in plain words.

Designed to protect

  • What you write, against our server, the network and anyone who later gets the server's storage, including someone who records traffic today and has a quantum computer later.
  • Old messages, if your device is taken later: a message key is deleted once it has been used.
  • Your conversations after a break-in: someone who stole a conversation's keys once and then lost access is locked out again within one round of messages.
  • Who sent a message: the server stores whom a waiting message is for, never whom it is from.
  • Your groups: the server holds no groups, group names or member lists.
  • What is stored on your device, against someone who has the device but not your passphrase.

Does not protect

  • Your IP address and timing. The server sees where every connection comes from, and someone watching it live can match senders and recipients. Use Tor or a VPN if that matters to you.
  • How much you receive and when.
  • Whether a username exists.
  • A stolen recovery phrase. Whoever has it can act as you.
  • Some recordings, for a limited time, if your device is taken: the first messages of some conversations for up to 40 days, and keys of messages that arrived out of order for up to 14 days.
  • A compromised device. Malware running as you reads what you read.

03Where LUME is today

Built in the open. Not released yet.

The specification is a draft, revision {{spec-revision}}, and can still change.

Built and tested

  • The cryptographic core in C++, on libsodium and liboqs, with a self-test that runs before anything else
  • Accounts from a 24-word recovery phrase
  • Safety numbers
  • The hybrid post-quantum handshake
  • The Double Ratchet with encrypted headers
  • The binary wire format, with a fuzz target for every parser

Not built yet

  • Message content and sealed sender
  • Encrypted storage on the device
  • The relay server
  • The Android app
  • The Windows app
  • An independent security audit

No app to download yet

There is no LUME app in any store and no download anywhere. Anything offered as LUME today is not from us.

The first apps will be for Android and Windows. There will be no web version.
