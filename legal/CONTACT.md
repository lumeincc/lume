## 4. What we cannot help with

1. We cannot read your messages. They are end-to-end encrypted, and we hold no
   key to decrypt them. The only exception is messages that a recipient
   reports to us: the report contains them.
2. We cannot recover a lost recovery phrase or passphrase, restore messages
   deleted from your device, or unlock your vault, because we never have your
   recovery phrase, your passphrase, your stored messages or the keys to your
   vault.
3. We cannot tell you who wrote to you, because the relay does not record the
   sender of a message.

What we do store is described in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

## 5. Authorities you can turn to

You may also turn to a public authority or a court at any time. In the
Republic of Kazakhstan these include the authorised body for personal-data
protection and the authorised body for consumer protection. In the European
Union they include the data protection supervisory authority of the country
where you live or work.
