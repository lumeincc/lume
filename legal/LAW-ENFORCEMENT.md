# LUME Law Enforcement Policy

**Status: draft.** Takes effect with the first public release.
Русская версия: [LAW-ENFORCEMENT.ru.md](LAW-ENFORCEMENT.ru.md).
Қазақша нұсқа: [LAW-ENFORCEMENT.kk.md](LAW-ENFORCEMENT.kk.md).

This page explains to public authorities and to users what LUME Inc. can and
cannot provide, and how.

## 1. What exists

Only the data in section 3 of the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) that relates to
a specific username:

- the username, the account's public keys, its generation number and its
  discoverability setting;
- the **day** the account was created and the **day** it last connected;
- how many delivery tokens the account has issued, and the days they were
  created and last used — but not to whom they were issued;
- records that the account's one-time keys were handed out over the last
  15 days — with nothing about who asked for them;
- how many encrypted messages and attachments are awaiting delivery to the
  account (for at most 30 days); we cannot decrypt them;
- reports by other users about messages from the account — for 90 days.

## 2. What does not exist

We do not have and cannot provide:

- the content of messages or attachments — we do not hold and have never held
  the keys to decrypt them;
- contact lists, or who an account talks to;
- the sender of any stored message;
- phone numbers, e-mail addresses, names or payment details — we do not
  collect them;
- IP address logs — addresses are used in memory and never recorded;
- group names or member lists — the server holds no groups.

**There is no backdoor, and we will not build one.** The cryptography is open,
and anyone can check that a backdoor would show in the code.

## 3. What a request must contain

We consider a request if it:

- comes from an authority empowered to make it by the law of the Republic of
  Kazakhstan, in the manner that law sets, or through a proper channel of
  international legal assistance;
- is in writing, signed by an authorised official, and states its legal basis,
  the case or proceeding number and the deadline for the answer;
- names a specific LUME username and specific data from section 1.

Requests that foreign authorities send to us directly are considered only if
complying does not breach the law of the Republic of Kazakhstan; as a rule we
ask that they come through international legal assistance.

## 4. How we respond

- Every request is checked for lawfulness and scope; we disclose no more than
  the law requires and challenge requests that are excessive or unfounded.
- On a preservation request we may preserve the data in section 1 until a
  proper demand arrives, but no longer than the law permits.
- Unless the law or a court forbids it, we notify the user before disclosing
  data, so they can seek legal help.
- In an emergency involving a threat to life or of serious harm to health, we
  may voluntarily disclose the data in section 1 to prevent it.

## 5. Transparency

At least once a year we publish how many requests we received, from which
countries, how many we answered and what data we provided — to the extent the
law allows.
