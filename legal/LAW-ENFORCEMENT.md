# LUME Law Enforcement Policy

**Status: draft.** Takes effect with the first public release.
Русская версия: [LAW-ENFORCEMENT.ru.md](LAW-ENFORCEMENT.ru.md).

This page tells authorities, and users, what LUME Inc. can and cannot provide.

## What exists

Only the data listed in section 3 of the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md), for a
given username:

- the username, the account's public keys and its discoverability setting;
- the **day** the account was created and the **day** it last connected;
- the number of delivery tokens it has issued — not who holds them;
- encrypted messages waiting for delivery to it, for at most 30 days, which we
  cannot decrypt.

## What does not exist

We do not have, and cannot produce:

- the content of any message, call or attachment — we never hold the keys;
- contact lists, or who an account talks to;
- the sender of any stored message;
- phone numbers, email addresses, names or payment details — we never collect
  them;
- IP-address logs — addresses are used in memory and never written down;
- group names or member lists — the server holds no groups.

**There is no backdoor and we will not build one.** The cryptography is public
and anyone can check that a backdoor would show in the code.

## How we respond

- We respond only to requests made under the law of the Republic of
  Kazakhstan, through the procedure that law requires, or to requests that
  reach us through a valid international legal assistance channel.
- We check every request for legal validity and scope, and disclose no more
  than it lawfully requires.
- Unless the law or a court forbids it, we tell the user concerned before
  disclosing anything, so that they can seek legal advice.
- In an emergency involving a risk of death or serious injury, we may disclose
  the data above voluntarily to prevent it.

## Transparency

We will publish, at least once a year, how many requests we received, from
which countries, and how many we answered and with what.

## Contact

The contact address for authorities will be published here before the first
release.
