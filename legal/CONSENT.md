# Consent to the Collection and Processing of Personal Data

**Status: draft.** The LUME app shows this text, with a link to the
[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md), before it creates an account. Software other
than the official apps must show it too before it registers an account
([Terms of Use](TERMS.md)). It takes effect with the first public release.

By pressing *I accept* when I create a LUME account, I freely, of my own will
and in my own interest, give LUME Inc. consent to the collection and
processing of my personal data on the terms below, under articles 7 and 8 of
Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 94-V "On Personal Data and Their
Protection" (the "Kazakh law"). I give the consent in electronic form, by
pressing *I accept* in the app before the account is created. The app then
sends, in the registration request signed with my account key, the number of
the version of this consent, the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use that I
accepted. The server keeps that number and the day as LUME Inc.'s record of my
consent, and records a later version I accept in the same way. If I register
an account with software other than the official app, that software must show
me this text and send the version first, and registering the account is my
consent. I was shown the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) before I accepted.

1. **Owner and operator:** LUME Inc. (ТОО «LUME Inc.»), BIN 260740032421,
   Republic of Kazakhstan, Temirtau, Karaganda Region. The person responsible
   for organising the processing of personal data can be reached at the
   address on the [contact page](CONTACT.md).
2. **Data subject:** the LUME user identified by the account's username,
   account identifier and identity key. LUME does not collect surnames, first
   names or patronymics.
3. **Data:**
   - my username and a random account identifier;
   - the account's public keys and their signatures: the identity keys, the
     device key and the keys used to start a conversation, with the time each
     was created;
   - the account's generation number and the discoverability setting;
   - the day the account was created and the day it last connected;
   - the number of the version of this consent, the Privacy Policy and the
     Terms of Use that I accepted, and the day I accepted it;
   - any measure on the account (restriction or suspension) and the day it
     ends;
   - the record of any measure LUME Inc. takes on the account, with the facts
     and the clause of the Terms of Use on which it is based, and the decision
     on any appeal;
   - notices from LUME Inc. waiting for my app: the kind of each notice and
     the day it was issued;
   - records that one-time keys were handed out, with the day;
   - hashes of the delivery tokens my app issues, with their state (live,
     blocked, or left over from a previous device) and the days they were
     created and last used;
   - hashes of my invite codes, with their expiry time;
   - encrypted messages awaiting delivery to me, each with the time it
     arrived, the generation it was sealed for and the hash of the token or
     invite it came with;
   - encrypted attachments awaiting download by me, with their size, a hash of
     their upload key, the downloads left and their expiry time;
   - when I write to a stranger without a delivery token from them or an
     invite, a hash of my network, under a key that changes every day, kept
     with that message;
   - reports by other users about my messages, containing those messages and
     my identity keys, sealed so that only LUME Inc. can open them, with the
     time each arrived, and their content once authorised staff of LUME Inc.
     open them;
   - after the account is deleted or my username is barred, a hash of the
     username;
   - where the law requires it, where a competent authority lawfully asks,
     where LUME Inc. deletes the account for, or reports, child sexual
     exploitation, or where it reports a threat to life, a preserved copy of
     the data above, and the record of authority requests about my account;
   - my IP address and the time of connection, which are used in memory only
     and never recorded.

   Details and retention periods are in sections 3 and 12 of the
   [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). What I send to LUME Inc., at the addresses on
   the [contact page](CONTACT.md) or by post, is processed as section 12 of the
   Privacy Policy describes, on the grounds given there, including the consent
   I give by sending it.
4. **Purposes:** creating and keeping the account and letting people find me
   and check my keys; recording my acceptance of this consent and the
   documents it comes with; delivering encrypted messages and attachments;
   sending me notices from LUME Inc.; recovering the account on a new device;
   protecting the service and its users from spam, flooding, account farming
   and impersonation; acting on reports and enforcing the
   [Terms of Use](TERMS.md); meeting legal requirements. My data is not used
   for advertising, analytics or profiling.
5. **Operations:** collection, recording, storage, use, transfer under items 6
   and 7, blocking, de-identification, erasure and destruction, by automated
   means; reports are also examined by authorised staff of LUME Inc.
6. **Transfer to third parties:**
   - to the hosting provider whose equipment runs the server, on the
     instructions of and under contract with LUME Inc.;
   - to other LUME users, as the service requires: whoever looks me up under
     item 8 receives my username, my public keys and the certificate that
     links them, which also shows my account identifier and generation
     number;
   - to public authorities of the Republic of Kazakhstan, and to foreign
     competent authorities and recognised bodies under item 7, in the cases
     and manner the law sets, including reports of child sexual exploitation
     and of threats to life;
   - to a successor of LUME Inc. that takes over the service and this
     consent's terms, after a notice in the app at least 15 calendar days
     before the transfer.
7. **Cross-border transfer:** no routine transfer. The server and the data are
   in a data centre in the Republic of Kazakhstan, and my app sends data there
   directly from wherever I am. My data may be transferred abroad only:
   - to a foreign competent authority, through international legal assistance
     under a treaty of the Republic of Kazakhstan;
   - to a foreign competent authority or a recognised body, when LUME Inc.
     reports child sexual exploitation or a threat to life, as the law
     requires or allows;
   - where another law binding on LUME Inc. requires it;
   - where I choose to send something to LUME Inc. through GitHub private
     vulnerability reporting, which GitHub, Inc. (USA) holds under its own
     terms;

   in each case only the data that the case requires, and only as article 16
   of the Kazakh law permits. Where article 16 requires my consent for such a
   transfer, I give it for these cases only.
8. **Publication in publicly available sources:** none, except that if I
   report a vulnerability and ask to be credited, the publication of the fix
   names me (section 12 of the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md)). Within LUME:
   - while discoverability is on, any LUME user can look me up by my username;
   - while it is off, only people who hold an invite from me or who are
     already my contacts can look me up by my username;
   - whether a username is taken can always be checked by anyone.

   I can turn discoverability on or off in the app at any time.
9. **Term:** from my acceptance until the account is deleted, by me, by LUME
   Inc. under the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md), or automatically after 365 days
   without connecting; after that, LUME Inc. keeps only what item 10 lists,
   for the retention periods in sections 3 and 12 of the
   [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md), in the cases the law allows without this
   consent.
10. **Withdrawal:** I may withdraw consent at any time by deleting the account
    in the app. The server then deletes the account's data at once, although
    the law allows up to 15 working days. Only the following is kept, for the
    periods in the Privacy Policy:
    - reports about my messages, which the server cannot link to an account;
    - the hash of my username, held for up to 31 days, or kept while a bar
      lasts if the name was barred for abuse;
    - messages and attachments I sent that are still waiting for delivery to
      others, which the server cannot link to me;
    - data that the law requires to be kept or that a competent authority has
      lawfully asked to be preserved;
    - what I sent to LUME Inc. outside the server, and the other data in
      section 12 of the Privacy Policy, for the periods given there.

    Withdrawal does not affect processing before it, or processing that the law
    allows without consent (article 9 of the Kazakh law). Under article 8(2) of
    the Kazakh law, consent cannot be withdrawn where that would contradict the
    law.
11. **My rights** are set out in section 9 of the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md),
    including the right to complain to the authorised body for personal-data
    protection of the Republic of Kazakhstan or to a court.

I confirm that I am at least 18.

This consent is published in English. Before I accept, the app shows, on the
screen where I accept, a short summary of its main terms, and of those of the
Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use, in Kazakh and Russian, for information.
The English text governs.
