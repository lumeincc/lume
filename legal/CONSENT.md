# Consent to the Collection and Processing of Personal Data

**Status: draft.** This text is shown in the LUME app when an account is
created; it takes effect with the first public release.
Русская версия: [CONSENT.ru.md](CONSENT.ru.md).
Қазақша нұсқа: [CONSENT.kk.md](CONSENT.kk.md).

By creating a LUME account and pressing *I accept*, I freely, of my own will
and in my own interest give consent to the collection and processing of my
personal data on the following terms. The consent is given in electronic form
and confirmed by a registration request signed with my account key.

1. **Owner and operator:** LUME Inc. (ТОО «LUME Inc.»), BIN 260740032421,
   Republic of Kazakhstan, Temirtau.
2. **Data subject:** the LUME user identified by the account's username and
   identity key. LUME does not collect surnames, first names or patronymics.
3. **Data:** username; the account's public keys (identity key, device key and
   the keys used to start a conversation); the account's generation number;
   the discoverability setting; the day the account was created and the day it
   last connected; records that one-time keys were handed out; hashes of
   delivery tokens and invite codes with the days they were created and used;
   encrypted messages and attachments awaiting delivery to me; the IP address —
   in memory only, never recorded. Details are in section 3 of the
   [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
4. **Purposes:** creating and maintaining the account, delivering encrypted
   messages and attachments, protecting the service and its users from abuse,
   meeting legal requirements.
5. **Operations:** collection, recording, storage, use, blocking,
   de-identification, erasure and destruction, by automated means.
6. **Transfer to third parties:** only to the hosting provider whose equipment
   runs the server, on the instructions of and under contract with LUME Inc.,
   and to public authorities in the cases and manner the law sets.
7. **Cross-border transfer:** none — the server and the data are in a data
   centre in the Republic of Kazakhstan.
8. **Publication in publicly available sources:** none. The username is
   visible to other LUME users searching for me by name, and only while
   discoverability is on; I can turn it off in the app.
9. **Term:** until the account is deleted, or deleted automatically after 365
   days without connecting, and then for the retention periods in the
   [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
10. **Withdrawal:** I may withdraw consent at any time by deleting the account
    in the app. The data is deleted at once, except what the law requires to
    be kept.

I confirm that I am at least 16, and, where the law of my country sets a
higher age for giving consent on my own, that I have reached that age or that
my legal representative agrees to my using LUME.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **To be filled in before release:** have a lawyer confirm that a consent in
> which the subject is identified by username and key, rather than by surname,
> first name and patronymic, meets article 8(4) of the Kazakh law "On Personal
> Data and Their Protection".
