# The LUME website: privacy and terms of use

**Status: in effect.** This version applies from the day it is published on
the website. The date of every version is recorded in the public history of
this page in our repository.

This page covers only the LUME website at <https://lumeincc.github.io/lume/>
(the "website"). The website is run by LUME Inc. (section 11). Sections 1 to 7
describe how the website treats your privacy. Sections 8 to 10 are the terms of
use of the website. The LUME apps, the relay and LUME accounts are covered by
the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md), the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) and the
[Consent to the processing of personal data](CONSENT.md), not by this page.

## 1. In short

- **Nothing is stored on your device.** The website sets no cookies and uses no
  local storage, session storage or any other browser storage. It has no
  consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to.
- **No analytics, trackers or ads**, no visitor counters and no browser
  fingerprinting.
- **One script**, `site.js`, served by the website. On the home page it
  animates the heading and the buttons at the top of the page: their text
  appears out of random characters. It does nothing else, stores nothing and
  sends nothing anywhere. If your system asks for reduced motion, the script
  does not run and the stylesheet turns off its transitions. Without the
  script the website works the same, and the text is there at once.
- **Nothing from other servers.** Fonts and images are served by the website.
  There are no CDNs, embedded videos, maps or social buttons. The Content
  Security Policy of every page allows resources only from the website, and
  your browser enforces it.
- **No forms.** The website asks you to type nothing and collects nothing.
- **No referrer.** When you follow a link to another site, your browser is told
  not to reveal which page you came from.

## 2. Cookies

There are none. The website sets no cookies, its own or anyone else's, and
stores nothing in your browser by any other means. It is in English only, so it
has no language to remember. Some older addresses of the website are plain
pages that send your browser on to the current address of the same page; they
store nothing either.

If the website ever needs to store something on your device, we will change
this page first, and anything that is not strictly necessary will be stored
only with your consent.

## 3. Your browser settings

There is no choice to make on the website, because it stores nothing on your
device. Your browser's own settings for cookies and site data apply as usual,
and clearing them changes nothing on the website. The website also works with
JavaScript turned off.

## 4. Hosting and what it receives

1. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). To
   send you a page, GitHub's server receives what your browser sends with any
   request: your IP address, the address of the page, the time of the request
   and your browser's user-agent string. GitHub logs the IP addresses of
   visitors to GitHub Pages sites for security purposes and processes them on
   its own account, under the
   [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
2. LUME Inc. receives no access logs and does not see visitors' IP addresses.
   When you open the website, your browser connects to GitHub's servers
   directly; those servers may be outside the Republic of Kazakhstan.
3. LUME Inc. itself collects no personal data through the website. Where the
   EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, our ground for having
   the website delivered by a hosting service is our legitimate interest in
   publishing information about LUME (GDPR article 6(1)(f)).

## 5. Links to other sites

1. The website links to GitHub, where the source code is, and to the websites
   of the libraries and tools that LUME uses. Nothing is loaded from those
   sites until you follow a link; once you do, their own terms and privacy
   policies apply.
2. LUME Inc. does not control other sites. A link is not an endorsement, and
   LUME Inc. is not responsible for the content, availability or privacy
   practices of a site it links to.

## 6. Your rights

1. LUME Inc. keeps nothing about visitors to the website, and the website keeps
   nothing in your browser. A request about your data will therefore usually
   be answered that we hold none.
2. You keep every right that the law gives you over your personal data. Under
   the law of the Republic of Kazakhstan and, where it applies to you, the
   GDPR, these include the rights to know whether we process data about you,
   to have it corrected or deleted, to object, and to seek protection from the
   authorised body for personal-data protection of the Republic of Kazakhstan,
   from the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live or work, or
   from a court.
3. Questions about data that GitHub processes go to GitHub. How to reach us is
   on the [contact page](CONTACT.md).

2. A change does not apply to the past. If the website ever needs to collect
