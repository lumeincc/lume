# LUME Child Safety Standards

**Status: draft.** Takes effect with the first public release.
Русская версия: [CHILD-SAFETY.ru.md](CHILD-SAFETY.ru.md).
Қазақша нұсқа: [CHILD-SAFETY.kk.md](CHILD-SAFETY.kk.md).

LUME Inc. has **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation
(CSAE)** — including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion
and trafficking. These standards bind every LUME user.

## Prohibited

Using LUME to create, request, store or share CSAM or links to it, or to
contact, solicit, exploit or endanger minors, is forbidden by section 5 of the
[Terms of Use](TERMS.md) and by law.

## Who may use LUME

LUME is for people aged 16 and over; where the law of a user's country sets a
higher age, that age applies. Accounts we learn belong to younger children are
deleted.

## How to report

- **In the app:** open the conversation and choose *Report*. The report gives
  LUME Inc. the selected messages as you see them, and the sender's identity
  keys. Messages are end-to-end encrypted, so a recipient's report is the only
  way we can see their content. Please report.
- **Without the app:** write to the child-safety contact below.
- If a child is in immediate danger, **contact the police first** — in
  Kazakhstan, call 102 or 112.

## What we do with a report

1. We review it first — CSAE reports before all others.
2. We delete the offending account and bar its username.
3. We keep the report and the account data we hold (listed in section 3 of the
   [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md)) for as long as the law requires.
4. We report confirmed CSAE to the competent law enforcement authorities of the
   Republic of Kazakhstan and, where another country is concerned, to the
   relevant national or international body, as the law requires.

## What end-to-end encryption means here

We cannot scan message content and will not add a way to read it: a loophole
for us would be a loophole for anyone who breaks into us. We can act on user
reports, on accounts and names, and on signs of abuse the server sees without
reading messages, such as mass account creation.
