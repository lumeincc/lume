# LUME Child Safety Standards

**Status: draft.** Takes effect with the first public release.
Русская версия: [CHILD-SAFETY.ru.md](CHILD-SAFETY.ru.md).

LUME Inc. has **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation
(CSAE)**, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion
and trafficking. These standards apply to everyone who uses LUME.

## Prohibited

Using LUME to create, request, store, share or link to CSAM, or to contact,
groom, exploit or endanger a minor in any way, is forbidden by our
[Terms of Use](TERMS.md) and by law.

## Who can use LUME

LUME is for people aged 16 and over. Accounts that we learn belong to younger
children are deleted.

## How to report

- **In the app:** open the conversation, choose *Report*. The report sends the
  reported messages, as you see them, and the sender's username to LUME Inc.
  Because messages are end-to-end encrypted, a report from a recipient is the
  only way we can see their content — please report.
- **Without the app:** write to the child-safety contact below.
- If a child is in immediate danger, **contact the police first.**

## What we do with a report

1. Review it promptly, giving CSAE reports priority over every other kind.
2. Delete the offending account and block its username from being registered
   again.
3. Preserve the report and the account data we hold (listed in our
   [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md), section 3) for the period the law requires.
4. Report confirmed CSAE to the competent law enforcement authority of the
   Republic of Kazakhstan, and to the relevant national or international
   reporting body where the case involves another country, as the law
   requires.

## What end-to-end encryption means here

We cannot scan message content, and we will not add a way to read it — a
backdoor for us would be a backdoor for anyone who breaks into us. What we can
act on is what users report, accounts and usernames, and abuse patterns the
relay can see without reading messages (for example mass account creation).

## Child-safety contact

The designated contact for child-safety matters and for authorities will be
published here before the first release.
