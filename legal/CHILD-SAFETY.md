# LUME Child Safety Standards

**Status: draft.** Takes effect with the first public release.

These are the published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation
of **LUME**, the messenger developed and run by **LUME Inc.** (ТОО «LUME Inc.»,
BIN 260740032421).

LUME Inc. has **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation
(CSAE)**, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion
and trafficking. These standards explain how the rules of use in section 5 of
the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md) apply to CSAE, and what LUME Inc. does about it.
They do not replace the Terms of Use.

## Prohibited

1. Under section 5 of the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md), you must not use LUME to:
   - create, request, store, share or advertise CSAM, or links to it;
   - contact, groom, solicit, coerce, sexually exploit or otherwise endanger a
     child;
   - threaten a child, or anyone, with the release of sexual images
     (sextortion);
   - arrange or promote the trafficking of children.
2. In these standards a child is a person under 18, whatever age of consent
   applies where you are.
3. Any of these is a serious breach of the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md). It can
   also be a crime under the law of the Republic of Kazakhstan and of other
   countries.

## Who may use LUME

LUME is for people aged 18 and over, in every country. If we learn that an
account belongs to a person under 18, we suspend it and tell the account in the
app. We delete it once the 30-day appeal period in section 6 of the
[Terms of Use](TERMS.md) has passed, or an appeal has been rejected.

## How to report

1. **In the app:** open the conversation, select the messages and choose
   *Report*. You do not leave the app. The report gives LUME Inc. the selected
   messages as you received them, within a size limit, and the sender's
   identity keys. Messages are end-to-end encrypted, so a report from a
   recipient is the only way we can see their content. The server does not
   record who sent a report, and we do not tell the reported account who
   reported it. Please report.
2. **Without the app:** anyone, including a parent or a person who does not use
   LUME, can write to the child-safety contact below. Give the username of the
   account and describe what happened. Do not send us the material itself.
3. Do not save, copy or forward CSAM to anyone, even to report it, other than
   through *Report* in the app. Doing so can itself be a crime.
4. If a child is in immediate danger, **contact the police first**. In
   Kazakhstan, call 102 or 112.

## What we do with a report

1. We open reports promptly, and we act first on those that show CSAE. The
   server cannot tell us what a report is about until we open it. People
   review reports; we use no automated tool to decide on a report.
2. If a report, or other information we hold, clearly shows CSAE, we first
   preserve the evidence: the report and the account data the server holds
   (listed in section 3 of the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md)).
3. Only after the evidence is preserved do we delete the offending account and
   bar its username, so that it cannot be registered again. We do this at once
   and without notice. We do not first suspend the account or wait for the
   appeal period.
4. If a report suggests CSAE but does not clearly show it, we may suspend the
   account while we assess it, and then act under section 6 of the
   [Terms of Use](TERMS.md).
5. We report apparent CSAE without undue delay to the competent law enforcement
   authorities of the Republic of Kazakhstan. Where another country is
   concerned, we may also report it to the competent authority or the
   recognised national body of that country, as the law requires or allows,
   and only where the law of the Republic of Kazakhstan on the cross-border
   transfer of personal data permits it. We give them the preserved evidence
   as described in the [Law Enforcement Policy](LAW-ENFORCEMENT.md).
6. We keep the preserved evidence only for the authorities and for as long as
   the law requires or a competent authority lawfully asks, and we then delete
   it. Only the staff who handle the report can access it, and we use it for no
   other purpose.
7. We answer requests from the competent authorities about CSAE as the
   [Law Enforcement Policy](LAW-ENFORCEMENT.md) describes.
8. The account holder may appeal the measure as section 6 of the
   [Terms of Use](TERMS.md) describes. A deleted account cannot be restored,
   but an appeal that succeeds lifts the bar on its username. An appeal does
   not stop us from preserving evidence or reporting to the authorities.

## What end-to-end encryption means here

1. We cannot scan message content. We will not add a way to read it
   voluntarily: a way in for us would also be a way in for anyone who breaks
   into our systems. We would contest any demand to add one by every lawful
   means.
2. We act on user reports, on accounts and usernames, and on signs of abuse
   that the server sees without reading messages, such as unusual numbers of
   registrations, which it limits automatically.
3. These standards cover the LUME relay that LUME Inc. runs. Anyone may run a
   relay of their own from the open-source code. We cannot see or act on such
   a relay, but we may pass a report about it to the competent authorities.

## Compliance with the law

1. LUME Inc. complies with the child-safety laws that apply to it, including
   the law of the Republic of Kazakhstan, and with the child safety standards
   of the app stores that distribute LUME.
2. Nothing in these standards limits what the law requires of LUME Inc., or the
   right of anyone to go to the police or a court.
3. We review these standards when the law or the service changes. The current
   version is the one published on our website, and its history is public in
   our repository.

## Child-safety contact

LUME Inc. designates a child-safety point of contact who can speak to
LUME's practices for preventing CSAM and for handling reports, and answers the
competent authorities and the app stores.
